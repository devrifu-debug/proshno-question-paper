## Buddhist (বৌদ্ধধর্ম শিক্ষা, Class 7)
- Ch3 p.23–25 (উপোসথ কাহিনি): the Bodhisattva is born in Benares and the rich man is said to live in রাজগৃহ, yet the Benares king walks past the rich man's house. The location is inconsistent.
- Ch3 p.26 lists the 8 paths with সম্যক প্রচেষ্টা before সম্যক স্মৃতি. Ch6 p.50 puts সম্যক স্মৃতি (6) before সম্যক ব্যায়াম (7). The order is inconsistent (the usual order is ব্যায়াম 6, স্মৃতি 7).
- Ch4 p.32 and Ch8 p.76 conflict about Anathapindika's journey. Ch4 says he built a vihara at every yojana over 45 yojanas. Ch8 says the distance was about 90 miles, with a rest house every 2 miles. The two chapters also disagree on what the 18 crore was spent on.
- Ch5 sample CQ: the stimulus has one student murdering a classmate. This is violent content for Class 7, and the গ question does not map to any specific gatha. The bank uses its own stimuli.
- Ch7 sample MCQ 3: the stimulus says শীল is kept "আষাঢ়ী থেকে কার্তিকী পূর্ণিমা", but the chapter defines উৎকৃষ্ট প্রতিহার্য উপোসথ as আষাঢ়ী → আশ্বিনী (3 months).
- Ch8 p.76: "বুদ্ধ রাজগৃহের জেতবনে অবস্থান করছিলেন" is wrong. Jetavana is in Shravasti, and the same page later says Anathapindika bought it in Shravasti.
- Ch8 sample MCQ 3 says "কপিল ব্রাহ্মণ ও ভদ্রা কপিলানির সংসার ত্যাগ". Kapila was the father; the husband was পিপ্পলী মানব.
- Ch9 sample CQ 2 (ঘটনা-১) builds the stimulus around mocking an elderly woman's squint, which is insensitive. The stimulus for MCQ 3–4 uses ideas (রাজধর্ম/লোকধর্ম পরায়ণ) that the chapter does not teach.
- Ch10 p.94 says Hiuen Tsang came to Nalanda in the "ষষ্ঠ শতাব্দী", but the same chapter (p.98) says he came in the 7th century, which is correct.
- Ch10 places রাজগৃহ in Patna district (it is now in Nalanda district) and শ্রাবস্তী in Gonda district (now Shravasti district). These are outdated.
- Ch11 p.102 says Ashoka's "রাজধানী ছিল মগধ". Magadha was the kingdom; the capital was পাটলিপুত্র (p.103 itself says পাটলিপুত্র).
- OCR could not confirm several garbled names (e.g. the vihara in Rajagriha in Ch7, the king's name in দেবধর্ম/মিত্রামিত্র জাতক). The bank avoids questions on them.
